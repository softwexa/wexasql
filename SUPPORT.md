# WexaSQL support

For installation, package compatibility, licensing and product support, [submit a WexaSQL support request](https://www.softwexa.com/support/?product=wexasql#support-request) or email [support@softwexa.com](mailto:support@softwexa.com?subject=WexaSQL%20support).

For Free access, follow the [license application guide](./FREE-LICENSE.md). Applications are handled through the private website form or hello@softwexa.com.

## Preparing a support report

Include the product name, package version when known, Dynamics 365 Finance application/platform versions, environment type, expected behavior, observed behavior and a minimal set of reproduction steps.

Share anonymized table and field names, a redacted error message and the relevant role or company context. Keep customer records, credentials, tenant identifiers, license keys and connection strings out of public issues.
